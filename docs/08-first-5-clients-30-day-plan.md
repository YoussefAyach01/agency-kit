# 30-day plan: your first 5 clients

Honest framing: 5 paying clients in 30 days is a **stretch target**, not a promise. A realistic outcome for a first month is 1 to 3 clients and a lot of learning. Every number below is a target you set, not a forecast. Nothing here requires spending money except your time (and a domain for the agency, optional).

## Rules

- Only public business contacts, one follow-up, stop at "no" (see outreach templates).
- Work on one niche first (the site-kit templates: restaurant, barber, dentist, gym). Pick the one you understand best.
- Log every conversation in a simple table: name, channel, date, answer, next step.

## Week 1: get ready (days 1 to 7)

- [ ] Day 1: decide the agency name and contact; fill the placeholders in `site/` (name, WhatsApp, email)
- [ ] Day 2: run site-kit, choose your niche, check the demo pages on your phone in FR and AR
- [ ] Day 3: verify domain/hosting prices (checklist in `06-domain-hosting-costs.md`)
- [ ] Day 4: get the Arabic templates proofread by a native speaker; get the contract reviewed by a lawyer (book the appointment even if it happens later)
- [ ] Day 5: deploy your own agency page (needs an account: your decision) and open a WhatsApp Business number
- [ ] Day 6: list 30 local businesses in your niche with no website (walk your neighbourhood, note names and public contacts)
- [ ] Day 7: rehearse the discovery call once with a friend

## Week 2: first conversations (days 8 to 14)

- [ ] Days 8 to 10: contact 10 of the 30, personalised, one per business (target: 10 messages, not 30 at once)
- [ ] Days 8 to 14: visit 5 businesses in person with your phone showing an example template; introduce yourself, leave a card or your WhatsApp
- [ ] Ask 3 people you know for an introduction to a shop owner (friends and family price for the first clients, in exchange for permission to show the result and a Google review)
- [ ] Book any discovery calls that come; use the checklist

## Week 3: propose and sign (days 15 to 21)

- [ ] Send proposals the same day as each call
- [ ] Follow up once on each unanswered message from week 2
- [ ] Contact the next 10 businesses
- [ ] Get the first deposit before starting any work
- [ ] Start building the first client site (target about 2 hours for Vitrine)

## Week 4: deliver and learn (days 22 to 30)

- [ ] Deliver client 1 with the handover checklist
- [ ] Ask client 1 for a Google review and permission to show the work, in writing
- [ ] Continue outreach to the last 10 businesses
- [ ] Track real hours per client and adjust the pricing sheet
- [ ] Day 30 review: messages sent, answers, calls, proposals, signed, paid. Write down what you would change. Decide the next 30 days.

## If nothing works by day 20

- Change one thing at a time: the niche, the first message, or the offer (for example a free one-page preview only for businesses that reply).
- Ask two owners who said no why. One honest sentence teaches more than another 20 messages.
- Consider partnering with a photographer or a print shop who already meets shop owners.
