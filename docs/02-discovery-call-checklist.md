# Discovery call checklist (15 to 20 minutes)

Goal: understand the business, pick the tier, leave with a next step. Do not sell during the first 10 minutes. Take notes in the client's language.

## Before the call (5 min)

- [ ] Find their Instagram / Facebook / Google Maps listing. Write down what they have and what is wrong (old hours, no prices, no link).
- [ ] Have the closest `site-kit` template open on your phone as an **example** (never claim it is already theirs).
- [ ] Ask permission to call: "Is now a good time for 15 minutes?"

## 1. The business

- [ ] Name, type, neighbourhood/city, how long open?
- [ ] Who are your customers? Locals, tourists, companies?
- [ ] How do customers find you today? (Instagram, word of mouth, Google Maps, passing by)
- [ ] What do customers ask most on WhatsApp/DM? (prices, hours, location, booking)

## 2. Current presence

- [ ] Website today? Who owns the domain and has the login?
- [ ] Google Business profile: claimed? Correct hours, phone, photos?
- [ ] Logo, colours, photos available? Who took them?
- [ ] Languages needed: French, Arabic, both, English?

## 3. What they want

- [ ] Main goal: more calls? more WhatsApp messages? bookings? look professional?
- [ ] Need online booking or a QR menu? (then the Sur mesure tier)
- [ ] Anything a competitor has that they liked?
- [ ] Deadline (Ramadan, season, opening date)?

## 4. Practical

- [ ] WhatsApp number for the site (a business number, not a private one they may change)
- [ ] Opening hours (including Friday and holidays), address, Maps pin
- [ ] Price list (items and MAD prices) ready, or to be collected?
- [ ] One person who approves the text.
- [ ] Who can answer me within 2 days during the build?

## 5. Budget and decision

- [ ] Have they paid for a website before? What happened?
- [ ] State the price of the fitting tier (see pricing sheet). Silence is fine.
- [ ] Who decides? Do they need to ask a partner?
- [ ] Next step with a date: proposal sent by ____ , answer by ____

## After the call (10 min)

- [ ] Send a short thank-you with the recap in 3 lines.
- [ ] Fill in the proposal (`04-proposal-template.md`) the same day.
- [ ] Note red flags: no time to give content, "same as X but free", unclear who decides.

## Do not

- Promise "first on Google", a number of visitors or sales. Nobody can promise that.
- Start work before the deposit is received.
- Accept content only as voice notes: ask for a written list.
