# Pricing sheet (MAD)

> All prices are **my own proposals**, in the same range as `site-kit/BUSINESS.md`. Adjust after your first two clients. "Setup" is paid once, "care" is the optional monthly plan. Prices are without domain: the domain is billed at cost (see `06-domain-hosting-costs.md`).

## The three tiers

| | **Vitrine** | **Pro** | **Sur mesure** |
|---|---|---|---|

| Based on | One `site-kit` template, one page | `site-kit` template, fully customised | `site-kit` + `booking-page` or `qr-menu` |

| Booking / QR menu | No | No | Yes, own deployment for this client |

| Revisions included | 1 round | 2 rounds | 2 rounds |

## Payment terms (proposal)

- 50% on signing, 50% before the site goes live on the client's domain.
- Care is paid monthly in advance, by whichever channels you actually use (cash, bank transfer, a mobile wallet). You choose.
- Do not hand over source files or transfer the domain before the final payment.

## Cost to you (rough)

- Domain: billed at cost to the client.
- Hosting for static sites: free tiers (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages) as of last check. Verify current terms before you promise anything.
- Your time: about 2 hours for Vitrine once the routine is known (estimate taken from `site-kit/BUSINESS.md`), 4 to 6 hours for Pro (my estimate).
- Below about 1,000 MAD a project you are working for almost nothing, so do not discount under it.

## How to talk about price

- Say the total first ("1,500 dirhams, one time, then 49 a month if you want me to keep it up to date"), then what is included.
- If they compare to a cousin at 500 MAD: you deliver in two languages, on their own domain, with a WhatsApp button, in days, and you answer when something breaks. Do not attack the cousin.
- A friends-and-family price for the first three clients is fine if they agree (in writing) to let you show the result and to leave a Google review.
